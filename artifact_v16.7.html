What to do & when
1 · Update sales actuals (roll the month forward)
Monthly, once sales are loaded. The app does not read actuals live.
Run extract_sku_data.gs in Apps Script → upload the sku_data.json it saves to this chat → ask to rebuild.
2 · Refresh Preorder & Key‑account orders
Anytime you change those Airtable tables.
BUY tab → Refresh data. Pulls live from Airtable — no rebuild.
3 · Forecasts in PLAN
Pre-loaded forecasts (current_forecasts) load on open; edit any cell to override.
Save Forecasts writes your view back to Airtable. The buy plan reflects edits live.
4 · Buy plan demand
No action needed.
Open BUY — it uses SKU-level forecasts (forecast_outputs) where available, otherwise your live PLAN forecast.
5 · Remove a SKU from the plans
Both plans mirror the Airtable product master — a SKU shows while it's available for that country/channel and not discontinued. The app doesn't read the master live, so a rebuild is needed to drop it.
Hide it now (view only): PLAN — keep the Active only pill on (hides discontinued SKUs). BUY — turn off the DISC status pill.
Remove it for good: in the product master set the SKU's discontinue date (a past date) for that country — or clear its channel availability / remove the SKU — then run extract_sku_data.gs → upload sku_data.json → ask to rebuild. Discontinuing drops it from PLAN; clearing availability also drops it from BUY.
6 · How new SKUs get a forecast
A SKU with little or no sales history still needs a number. Two paths, in order:
a) Replacement SKU — if the product master sets a replacement_sku, the new SKU inherits that predecessor's sales history as its forecast basis. Applied during extract_sku_data.gs, so it needs the usual extract → rebuild.
b) Subcategory contribution — otherwise the SKU takes a share of its subcategory forecast, scaled by its market tier. Tiers are weighted A / B / C (default 3 / 2 / 1): an A-tier SKU is seeded at 3× the weight of a C-tier one. This is what the BUY plan, the SKU drill-down and the inline expander all use, so a brand-new SKU shows a non-zero number everywhere.
Tier weights:
Only affects SKUs with no history and no replacement. Equal weights (e.g. 1/1/1) gives a flat split; resets to 3/2/1 on reload.
7 · Smoothing (the “smooth” gap button)
When a subcategory's SKU rows don't add up to its forecast, a gap shows on the “↳ N total” line. Click smooth on a month to rescale that month's SKUs so they sum to the subcategory forecast.
It distributes the subcategory target across SKUs in proportion to each SKU's current forecast (a capped proportional fill), with these rules:
  • Pre-launch — a SKU gets 0 for any month before its launch date.
  • Discontinued — past its discontinue date a SKU is capped by remaining stock (on-hand + inbound, run down month by month); it’s never allocated more than it can ship.
  • Black Friday (November) — in November, a discontinued SKU that still has stock is pushed to clear inventory: its weight is set to last November’s actuals + 50% (still capped by remaining stock).
Smoothing writes per-SKU overrides — Save Forecasts persists them. It never exceeds the subcategory total or a discontinued SKU’s stock.
Refresh data & Save write to Airtable directly. Actuals need a rebuild because they're embedded in the app.
