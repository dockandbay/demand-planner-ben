## 5. What changed (v27.170 → v27.222)

**Price Lists (v27.192–222)** — full feature: admin grid (positional Main/Secondary/Tertiary supplier cols, Size/Colour, MASTER-only, MANUAL section, exclude list, tiers, from-production versioning), PO estimated-cost wiring (v27.204), Phase-2 approval workflow (v27.205, pending→approve/reject, red counter badge), Phase-3 gated portal (v27.208+), per-price-point change logs both sides, portal deep link, and an **Excel (.xlsx) export** — two sheets (Price types · SKUs) with Category (v27.219–220). exceljs (already a dep) generates it server-side.

**Demand-plan bulk-save reliability (v27.221–222) — NO migration.** A smoothing sweep saved thousands of cells as *one query per row in a single long transaction*, holding one pool connection open long enough to time out. Now: server does **batched multi-row upserts** (~500–1000/query, de-duped, 120s statement guard) in `save-sku-forecasts` / `save-forecasts` / `changes/bulk`; client sends big saves in **serialised chunks (5,000 rows, one in flight at a time)** with **exponential backoff (1/2/4…30s)** and a **3-failure circuit breaker** (pauses, leaves dirty for the next autosave). Also fixed: `changes/bulk` silently capped the audit trail at 5,000 rows (reported the full count) — now loops the insert and reports the true count. *Behaviour identical (same upserts/clears), just far lighter on the pool.*
  - **Note (Diviyaj):** you flagged the **drift-query cache** as the real cause of connection starvation — agreed, and that's in the harness you own (no such query in Ben's server/artifact). This batch is the app-side hardening; the drift-query cache is yours to lead as the top priority.
