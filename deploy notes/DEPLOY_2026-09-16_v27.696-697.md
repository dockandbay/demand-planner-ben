## v27.696 — 3PL invoice (EU iFulfilment): stop double-counting Consumables (SERVER + CLIENT)
- **Bug:** Aug-26 EU reconcile grand total read EUR 47,531.37 vs invoice INV-192928 net 46,190.59 — over by exactly the Consumables line (1,340.78).
- **Cause:** from the Aug-26 export iFulfilment's "Total Excl Shipping" column already INCLUDES "Consumables Cost" (Jul-26 and earlier it did not). The v27.258 fold that added consumables on top now double-counted.
- **Fix:** `_tplConsumablesInside(headers, sumOf)` decides per order sheet from the column sums and skips the fold when consumables are already inside. Applied to both the Analyse grand total (`_tplGridSummary`) and the per-order fulfilment used by map / journal / Xero bill (`_tplOrderRows`). Parsed summary shows a per-sheet "Consumables: N (inside / outside)" note.
- **Verified** on both live workbooks: Jul-26 unchanged (48,259.51), Aug-26 = 46,190.59 = invoice net. Other 3PLs have no such column → unchanged.
- **Interim:** Ben has the corrected Aug-26 Xero bill + journal CSVs already (generated from the fixed sandbox against mirrored live orders), so nothing to re-run after deploy unless the file is re-analysed.
