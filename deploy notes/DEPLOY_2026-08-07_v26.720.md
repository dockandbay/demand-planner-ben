## What changed (v26.658 → v26.720)

**Multi-currency (v26.676–682, 687–688)** — CONFIG ▸ Admin ▸ Exchange rates (per-FY, inherit forward to FY2040, show current+2y); a single global GBP/Local toggle in the DEMAND nav that follows the market (US=USD, EU=EUR, AU=AUD, CA=CAD); applied to Summary, plan grid cells/ASP/totals, revenue popup, and Cash Flow.

**Summary / Set-targets (v26.664–675, 700, 705–710; mig 198)** — enter % growth OR a £ target (auto-detect, green/blue, £→units via retail pricing); category multi-select filter; sticky toolbar/headers; target-recommendations drawer (counters, per-row apply, dismiss, month table); hover breakdown tooltip (baselines on **last-year actuals**, flips up, left-aligned, pinnable/copyable, 300ms); LY row on Rev+targets; edit-mode ignores the month-range filter; instant £/% reformat on entry.

**DEMAND cell indicators + notes + do-not-smooth (v26.690–704; app_settings smooth_locks)** — vertical indicator rail (i = BI rule, C = calendar, N = note, S̶ = do-not-smooth) at category + SKU level; do-not-smooth engine (SKU-only; locked cells held fixed, others fill the subcat target); double-click notes at SKU + category (dd-mmm-yy dates); global 120ms tooltips app-wide; large growth % abbreviated to ±Nk%; override input = right 50% of the cell; FY-boundary border fix. **Includes a critical fix** (v26.704): `dateDMY` was out of scope in the note code and blanked the whole plan — resolved.

**Record of change "R" (v26.711–712, 718; mig 199)** — per-cell audit log (who/what/when) for manual edits, smoothing, target-recommendation apply, and do-not-smooth lock; rail "R" (only when the More-Filters "Show record of change" toggle is on) → click for descending history; "Hide for past months" toggle (default on); manual edits batched into the 60s autosave.

**Manufacturing (v26.659, 683, 685–686, 688–689)** — MFG POs limited to their own production number; prominent demand chip + carry-back hint; drawer full columns with editable stock + inline sign-off bound to the main table; quick-apply a supplier-submitted completion date under the PO END cell.

**Invoice & packing (v26.707–708, 713–717; mig 200)** — buttons renamed "… & Packing List"; packing-list tab now shows product SIZE + a Carton Dimensions column with cartons to 1dp (SUG-0021); **Direct to Client Packing List** (per-PO + consolidated-on-shipment) — seller = Dock & Bay (entity per key account, default UK), invoice no = client sales-order ref (+ client PO), consignee + notify party = the client, "Carton Size" column.

**Supplier portal (v26.719)** — hides archived (completed, pre-cutoff) POs by default with a "🗄 Show archived" toggle (big payload cut for large suppliers).

**Permissions (v26.658, 682, 720)** — Zalando stock upload, Klaviyo BIS upload, and **Quality Control file upload** (incl. metafields) are all allowed with read-only permission.

**PO grid / misc (v26.660–663, 684)** — required-field actions for in-production POs; "Master data missing" exceptions option; Payments-Due likely-pay-date binding; hide Paid-USD once a payment is assigned; PLAN action counter rolls up to the PURCHASE ORDERS menu; PO drawer respects the tab you click while detail loads.
