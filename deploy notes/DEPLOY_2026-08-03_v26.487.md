## ⚠️ SERVER change (1) — needs your eye
**v26.486** edits `server.mjs` `buildSKURAW()` — the **inbound feed query**:
- Now `LEFT JOIN planner.shipments sh ON sh.shipment_ref = po.shipment_ref` and `LEFT JOIN planner.branches b ON b.name = po.branch`.
- Adds a `po_eta` column = the PO-grid (into-stock) date: `coalesce(sh.arrival_date, sh.delivery_date, sh.landing_date, po.landing_date_overide, <calc landing: prod_end + 7 + branch sea transit>)` — same basis as the SUPPLY PO view.
- `po_eta` is carried onto each inbound line item (`i[...]` array).

Purely additive to the query output; the existing `eta` (estimated_delivery_date) is unchanged. If your hardened rehost has a divergent `buildSKURAW`, port this join + column across.

## ⚠️ BUY-PLAN IMPACT — overdue inbound (v26.486)
The buy engine was **dropping inbound whose ETA is in the past but which isn't yet received** (it bucketed by ETA month and the forward scan skips past months) → phantom stockout → **false urgent** (often urgent AIR). Fixed: inbound arrival date now resolves **future `estimated_delivery_date` → else the PO-grid date (`po_eta`) → else this month**, and is clamped into the first month the projection reads, so overdue-but-unreceived stock is counted.

- Snapshot (my sandbox) before→after: SKUs firing urgent **277→30**, Buy-3PL **69,418→23,732u**. **Sandbox is inflated by stale past dates** — live impact is limited to genuinely-overdue POs. Worth a spot-check on live post-deploy.
- This ties into your First-Buy flag below (fewer false urgents, but nothing changes new-launch under-buy).
