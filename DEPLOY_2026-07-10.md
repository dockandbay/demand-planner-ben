## CRITICAL -- Migration 109 (planning scope) and the product sync

An overnight n8n product sync was clobbering `in_planning_scope` to false for every row, emptying BUY/FBA
on live. Migration 109 makes it DERIVED: a BEFORE INSERT/UPDATE trigger recomputes
`coalesce(variant_type='MASTER' AND (any available_<country>_<channel> IS TRUE), false)` on every write,
plus a one-time backfill. A BEFORE trigger (not a generated column) so the sync can keep the column in its
upsert list without erroring. After applying 109 the sync no longer needs to set `in_planning_scope`. The
`coalesce(...,false)` guard is essential (a null variant_type otherwise violates NOT NULL and fails the
sync) -- use the committed 109. This supersedes Ben's earlier manual `status='ACTIVE'` stop-gap.
