## ⚠ CRITICAL — Migration 109 (planning scope) and the product sync

**Background:** an overnight n8n product sync was clobbering `in_planning_scope` to `false` for every row,
which emptied the BUY plan + FBA tab on live (it happened at least once). Ben manually restored it as a
stop-gap, but the next sync re-breaks it.

**What 109 does:** `in_planning_scope` becomes **derived** — a `BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE` trigger recomputes
it as `coalesce(variant_type='MASTER' AND (any available_<country>_<channel> IS TRUE), false)` on every row
write, plus a one-time backfill. A `BEFORE` trigger (not a generated column) is used on purpose so the sync
can keep the column in its upsert list without erroring — the trigger just overrides the value.

**After applying 109:**
- The product sync **no longer needs to set `in_planning_scope`** — the trigger owns it. This permanently
  fixes the overnight-sync outage.
- Per-country/channel scope is unaffected: `v_product_availability` still derives per-channel availability
  from the same `available_*` columns.
- The `coalesce(…, false)` guard is essential: without it, a null `variant_type` yields
  `(null='MASTER') AND true = null`, which violates the `NOT NULL` constraint on write and **would fail the
  sync**. Use the committed version of 109 (it has the guard).

**Note:** this new rule (`MASTER AND available`) supersedes Ben's earlier manual stop-gap
(`status='ACTIVE'`). Apply 109 and drop the manual approach.
