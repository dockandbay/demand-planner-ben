| 3 | Click **+ Add sample 添加样品**. Fill Sample date, tick Size variant and Aspects sampled. | To add a new sample, click Add sample. Enter the sample date, the sizes, and what this sample is for, for example colour or quality. | 要添加新样品，请点击“添加样品”。填写样品日期、尺码以及本样品的用途，例如颜色或质量。 |
| 4 | Tick the two checks (colour against Pantone, quality checked). Add a photo and a note. Click **Submit sample version**. | Before you submit, check the colour against the Pantone reference and check the quality, then tick both boxes. | 5 | On the new version row, click **⤓ Sample card 样品卡**. Show the A4 PDF; zoom to the QR code and 3-character code. | Every sample needs its card. Click Sample card to download it. | 6 | Hands printing the card and attaching it to a towel sample (real footage or a still). | Print the card and attach it to the sample, so our team can scan it the moment it arrives. | 打印样品卡并贴在样品上，这样样品一到，我们的团队就能立即扫描。 |
| 7 | SAMPLES > **Sample shipments 样品发运** > **+ New Sample Shipment 新建样品发运**. Fill recipient and completion dates. | When the samples are ready to send, go to Samples and click New sample shipment. Enter who it is going to and the dates. | 样品准备寄出时，请进入“样品”，点击“新建样品发运”，填写收件人和日期。 |
| 8 | Click **⌗ Add via QR code**. The camera opens; scan the first card. The sample appears under **Added to this batch** and the count reads 1. | Now add the samples by scanning. Click Add via QR code and point the camera at the card. The sample is added straight away. | 接下来扫描添加样品。点击“Add via QR code”，将摄像头对准样品卡，样品会立即添加。 |
| 8b | **Fast-forward (about 4x, a small ⏩ in the corner):** a stack of 20+ cards scanned one after another, the list growing and the **Added to this batch** counter climbing to 24. | 8c | Type a code (e.g. 9EK) and click **Look up** for a card the camera can't read. | 9 | Click **Create**. Then in the shipment: Carrier, **Tracking code**, **Save 保存**. | Create the shipment, then add the carrier and tracking code as soon as it is sent. Please always add the tracking code, even when it ships on Dock & Bay's courier account. | 创建发运后，寄出时请立即填写承运商和追踪号。即使使用 Dock & Bay 的快递账户寄送，也请务必填写追踪号。 |
| 10 | Short phone clip (Dock & Bay side, general): a team member scans a sample card with a phone, taps **✓ Mark received**, picks a decision, types a short comment. No names. | When your samples arrive, our product team scans each card on a phone, marks it received and records their feedback on colour and quality. | 样品到达后，我们的产品团队会用手机扫描每张样品卡，标记为已收到，并记录对颜色和质量的反馈。 |
| 11 | Back in the portal: product > Sample versions, the row now shows **💬 Feedback from Dock & Bay 💬 Dock & Bay 的反馈**. | You will see our feedback in the portal, on that sample version. If we ask for changes, make a new sample and add it as the next version. | 您会在门户中该样品版本下看到我们的反馈。如果我们要求修改，请制作新样品并作为下一个版本添加。 |
| 12 | Same row showing the decision **Approved for bulk**. | When we are happy, we mark the sample Approved for bulk. That is your signal that the sample is signed off for production. | 当我们满意时，会将样品标记为“Approved for bulk”，表示该样品已确认可以进入大货生产。 |
| 13 | Slow zoom out on the product page. | So: add each sample, print and attach its card, scan it into a shipment, add tracking, and watch for our feedback. Thank you for working with us. | 总结：添加每个样品，打印并贴上样品卡，扫描加入发运，填写追踪号，并留意我们的反馈。感谢您的合作！ |
