# Video 1: Using the Dock & Bay supplier portal (everything except PRODUCT)

Refreshed 05-Nov-26 for the current portal (v28.72x). Replaces the 02-Nov-26 draft. Supplier on screen: ACME Ltd (sandbox data only).
Narration: English, one clear voice (Gemini TTS). Subtitles: Simplified Chinese. Target about 6 minutes (about 760 words).
Screen recording of the real portal at 1920x1080, cursor visible, slow movements. Labels in **bold** are the exact on-screen text.
PRODUCT (product development and sample versions) is Video 2.

| # | Screen (what we record) | Narration (English) | 字幕 (中文) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Portal home, ACME Ltd signed in. Slow pan over the home cards and the top bar. | Welcome to the Dock & Bay supplier portal. This video shows how to manage your orders, productions, shipments, labels, payments and sample requests. | 欢迎使用 Dock & Bay 供应商门户。本视频介绍如何管理订单、生产批次、发运、标签、付款和样品需求。 |
| 2 | Hover the top pills **ORDERS**, **FINANCE**, **SAMPLES**, then the **中文** switch and the inbox. | Everything starts from the top bar. You can switch the whole portal to Chinese at any time, and messages from our team arrive in your inbox. | 所有功能都从顶部栏开始。您可以随时将整个门户切换为中文，我们团队的消息会出现在收件箱中。 |
| 3 | Home: the action cards (e.g. "to approve", "shipments need action"). Click one. | The home page lists what needs your attention. Click any card and it takes you straight to those items. | 首页列出需要您处理的事项。点击任意卡片即可直接进入相关内容。 |
| 4 | ORDERS > **Purchase Orders** grid. | Under Orders, Purchase Orders lists every open order, with its production, dates and destination. | 在“订单”中，“采购订单”列出所有未完成订单及其生产批次、日期和目的地。 |
| 5 | Click a PO: the drawer opens. Show the amber confirm banner, click **✓ Confirm order**; it turns green. | Click an order to open it. When we send a new or changed order, check the SKUs, quantities and dates, then click Confirm order. | 点击订单即可打开。当我们发送新订单或修改订单时，请核对 SKU、数量和日期，然后点击“确认订单”。 |
| 6 | Drawer > Order plan: **STEP 1 · ORDER PLAN** sub-tab. Point at the SKU, quantity and cost columns, then click **Next →**. | The order plan has two steps. In step one, check or update the SKUs, quantities and costs of the order. Then click Next. | 订单计划分两步。第一步，核对或更新订单的 SKU、数量和成本，然后点击“下一步”。 |
| 7 | **STEP 2 · INVOICE**: invoice amount (defaults to the order plan total), attach the commercial invoice and packing list, submit. | In step two, enter your invoice. The amount starts from the order plan total. Attach your commercial invoice and packing list and submit them for our approval. | 第二步，填写您的发票。金额默认为订单计划合计。附上商业发票和装箱单，提交给我们审批。 |
| 8 | Drawer > Timeline: **Completion date** picker, choose a date; show "awaiting Dock & Bay approval". | If your production completion date changes, update it here as soon as you know. It goes to our team for approval. | 如果生产完工日期有变化，请一得知就在这里更新。更新会提交给我们的团队审批。 |
| 9 | ORDERS > **Productions**: the production grid; under a PO the small **APPROVE** button; the **POs to approve** batch view. | Productions shows your orders grouped by production batch. When a production needs your approval, use the Approve button under the order. The POs to approve view lists them all in one place. | “生产批次”按批次显示您的订单。需要您批准时，请点击订单下方的“APPROVE”按钮。“待批准采购订单”视图会集中列出所有待批准订单。 |
| 10 | ORDERS > **Shipment Plan**, filtered to **Needs action**. Open a shipment. | The Shipment Plan shows each shipment and the orders travelling on it. Start with the shipments that need action. | “发运计划”显示每个发运及随其运输的订单。请先处理需要操作的发运。 |
| 11 | Shipment drawer > **Shipment, POs on board & labels**: the POs on board list, each PO row with its label buttons. Tick a PO on board. | On the shipment you see every order on board. Tick each order as it is loaded. Each order's labels are on its own row, ready to download. | 在发运中可以看到所有在运订单。每个订单装货后请勾选。每个订单的标签都在该订单所在行，可直接下载。 |
| 12 | Click **⤓ Download barcodes ▾**: **Product barcodes**, **Carton barcodes**, **Inner barcodes**; then **⤓ Shipment labels** and the pallet labels. | Download the product, carton and inner barcodes and the shipment labels from here. Pallet labels use the number of pallets for the shipment. Always use the labels from the portal, they are kept up to date. | 在这里下载产品条码、外箱条码、内盒条码和发运标签。托盘标签按此发运的托盘数生成。请始终使用门户中的标签，它们会保持最新。 |
| 13 | **📝 Update this shipment**: Mode / Carrier, **Tracking code**, **Ship date**, number of pallets. Show the highlighted tracking action. | Keep the shipment up to date: carrier, tracking code, ship date and the number of pallets. When the tracking code is missing it is highlighted, so it is easy to see what to fill in. Shipments booked with Flexport fill the carrier and tracking in automatically. | 请保持发运信息最新：承运商、追踪号、发运日期和托盘数。缺少追踪号时会高亮显示，方便您看到需要填写的内容。通过 Flexport 预订的发运会自动填入承运商和追踪号。 |
| 14 | **💰 Freight charges**: type a freight cost and description, add it; show it pending review. | If there are freight or handling costs, add them here with a short description. We review each one, and once accepted it becomes a payment to you. | 如有运费或处理费，请在这里填写并附上简短说明。我们会逐一审核，接受后将作为付款支付给您。 |
| 15 | FINANCE: **Deposits** and **Payments** tabs, a PO's **Total invoice value**, **Amount paid**, due dates; **📄 Tax Invoice** download. | Finance shows your deposits and payments for every order: the invoice value, what has been paid and when the next payment is due. You can also download the tax invoice. | “财务”显示每个订单的定金和付款：发票总值、已付金额以及下一笔付款的到期日。您也可以下载税务发票。 |
| 16 | SAMPLES > **Sample shipments**, **Open** filter. Click a requested sample; **✓ Confirm sample request**. | When we request samples, they appear under Samples. Open the request, check the SKUs and quantities, and confirm it. | 我们申请样品时，会显示在“样品”中。打开需求，核对 SKU 和数量，然后确认。 |
| 17 | Set status Shipped, Carrier, **Tracking code**, **Save**; add sample charges. | When the sample is sent, set it to shipped and enter the carrier and tracking code. Please always add the tracking code, even when it ships on Dock & Bay's courier account. Add any sample costs here too. | 样品寄出后，请设为已发运并填写承运商和追踪号。即使使用 Dock & Bay 的快递账户寄送，也请务必填写追踪号。如有样品费用，也请在这里填写。 |
| 18 | Back to the home page, slow zoom out. | That is it. Confirm your orders, keep the order plan, dates and shipments up to date, download your labels from the portal, and add tracking to every shipment and sample. If you have a question, message us from the portal. Thank you. | 以上就是全部内容。请确认订单，保持订单计划、日期和发运信息最新，从门户下载标签，并为每个发运和样品填写追踪号。如有问题，请通过门户联系我们。谢谢！ |

## Notes for Ben
- Riders on a shipment (not its owner) do not see the PO ticks; the demo shipment will have ACME as owner.
- Scene 5 only shows on POs where Dock & Bay requires confirmation; the demo PO will have it on.
- Scene 13: Flexport auto-fill only applies to Flexport-linked shipments; the demo shows a manual one.
- Sandbox data to prepare before recording: one ACME PO needing confirmation with an order plan, one production with a PO to approve, one shipment needing action (owner ACME), one requested sample.
