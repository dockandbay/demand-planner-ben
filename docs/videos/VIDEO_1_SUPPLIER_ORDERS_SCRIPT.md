# Video 1: Managing your orders in the Dock & Bay supplier portal

Draft for Ben's approval, 02-Nov-26. Supplier on screen: ACME Ltd (sandbox data only). Narration: English, one clear voice (Gemini TTS). Subtitles: Simplified Chinese.
Target length: about 5 minutes (about 640 words of narration). Screen recording of the real portal at 1920x1080, mouse cursor visible, slow movements.

Labels in **bold** are the exact on-screen text.

| # | Screen (what we record) | Narration (English) | 字幕 (中文) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Portal home, ACME Ltd signed in. Slow pan over the top bar. | Welcome to the Dock & Bay supplier portal. In this video we will show you how to manage your purchase orders, shipments, labels and sample requests. | 欢迎使用 Dock & Bay 供应商门户。本视频将介绍如何管理采购订单、发运、标签和样品需求。 |
| 2 | Hover each pill: **ORDERS 订单**, **FINANCE 财务**, **SAMPLES 样品**, **PRODUCT 产品**, **PROFILE 资料**. Point at the **中文** language switch and the Inbox button. | Everything is in the top bar. Orders, Finance, Samples and Product. You can switch the portal to Chinese at any time here, and new messages from our team arrive in your inbox. | 所有功能都在顶部栏：订单、财务、样品和产品。您可以随时在这里切换为中文，我们团队的新消息会出现在收件箱中。 |
| 3 | ORDERS > **Purchase Orders 采购订单** grid. | Start with Orders, then Purchase Orders. This list shows every open order with its production status, dates and destination. | 首先点击“订单”，然后选择“采购订单”。列表显示所有未完成订单的生产状态、日期和目的地。 |
| 4 | Click **MANAGE** on a PO. Drawer opens on **TIMELINE**. | To work on an order, click Manage. The order opens on the right, with tabs for the timeline, the order plan and invoice, payments and documents, the shipment, and barcodes and labels. | 要处理订单，请点击“MANAGE”。订单会在右侧打开，包含时间线、订单计划与发票、付款与文件、发运、条码与标签等标签页。 |
| 5 | Amber banner "Please confirm this order." Click **✓ Confirm order**, then confirm the dialog. Banner turns green. | When we send you a new order, or change one, you will see this banner. Check the SKUs, quantities and dates, then click Confirm order. The banner turns green to show the order is confirmed. | 当我们发送新订单或修改订单时，会出现此提示。请核对 SKU、数量和日期，然后点击“确认订单”。提示变为绿色，表示订单已确认。 |
| 6 | TIMELINE: click the **完工日期 Completion date** picker, choose a new date. Show "⏳ Submitted, awaiting approval". | If the completion date changes, update it here. Pick the new date and it saves automatically. Our team then reviews and approves the change, so please update it as soon as you know. | 如果完工日期有变化，请在这里更新。选择新日期后会自动保存。我们的团队会审核并批准该变更，所以一旦得知请尽快更新。 |
| 7 | **BARCODES & LABELS** tab. Click **⤓ Download barcodes for PO**. Show the ZIP and one opened PDF sheet. | Before packing, open Barcodes and labels. Download barcodes for PO gives you a zip file with an A4 PDF for every SKU: product labels, inner labels and carton labels, ready to print. | 包装前，请打开“条码与标签”。点击“下载采购订单条码”，会得到一个压缩文件，内含每个 SKU 的 A4 PDF：产品标签、内箱标签和外箱标签，可直接打印。 |
| 8 | Same tab: point at **⤓ Download custom barcodes** and **⤓ Download shipment labels** (only if shown on the demo PO). | Some orders also have custom barcodes or shipment labels. When they apply to your order, the buttons appear here. Always use the labels from the portal, as they are kept up to date. | 部分订单还有自定义条码或发运标签，适用时按钮会显示在这里。请始终使用门户中的标签，因为它们会保持最新。 |
| 9 | ORDERS > **Shipment Plan 发运计划**. Open a shipment card. | Next, the Shipment Plan. Each card is one shipment, with the purchase orders travelling on it. Click a shipment to open it. | 接下来是“发运计划”。每张卡片代表一个发运，以及随该发运运输的采购订单。点击发运即可打开。 |
| 10 | **📝 Update this shipment 更新此发运**: type Carrier, Tracking code, pick Ship date, set Status to Shipping. | Keep this panel up to date. Enter the carrier, the tracking code and the ship date, and when the goods leave your factory set the status to Shipping. Changes save automatically. | 请保持此面板信息最新。填写承运商、追踪号和发运日期；货物离开工厂时，将状态设为“Shipping”。更改会自动保存。 |
| 11 | **💰 Freight & Handling Charges 运费及处理费**: type an Amount and Description, click **Add charge**. Show "Pending review". | If there are freight or handling costs, add them here with a short description. Our team reviews each charge, and once it is accepted it becomes a payment. | 如有运费或处理费，请在这里填写金额和简短说明。我们的团队会审核每项费用，接受后将转为付款。 |
| 12 | Back in the PO drawer: **SHIPMENT** tab, click **Open shipment ↗**: the same shipment opens as a popout. | You can also reach the shipment from any order. On the Shipment tab, click Open shipment and it opens right here. | 您也可以从任意订单进入发运。在“发运”标签页点击“Open shipment”，发运会直接在此打开。 |
| 13 | SAMPLES > **Sample shipments 样品发运**, **Open** filter. Click a sample row; drawer opens. | Now, samples. When we request samples from you, they appear under Samples. Click a request to open it. | 接下来是样品。我们向您申请样品时，会显示在“样品”中。点击需求即可打开。 |
| 14 | Amber banner. Click **✓ Confirm sample request**. Show "✓ Sample request confirmed". | First check the SKUs and quantities, then click Confirm sample request so we know you have it. | 请先核对 SKU 和数量，然后点击“确认样品需求”，让我们知道您已收到。 |
| 15 | Set production status to **Shipped**, choose **Carrier** DHL, type **Tracking code**, click **Save 保存**. Status chip changes to Shipped. | When the sample is sent, set the status, choose the carrier and enter the tracking code, then click Save. Please always enter the tracking code, even when the sample ships on Dock & Bay's courier account. It is how we know the sample is on its way. | 样品寄出后，请设置状态、选择承运商并填写追踪号，然后点击“保存”。即使使用 Dock & Bay 的快递账户寄送，也请务必填写追踪号，这样我们才知道样品已在途中。 |
| 16 | Charges: type Freight and Product amounts and a Note, click **Create charge 创建费用**. | If the sample has a cost, add it here: freight, product and a short note, then click Create charge. We review it like any other charge. | 如果样品产生费用，请在这里填写运费、产品费用和简短备注，然后点击“创建费用”。我们会像其他费用一样进行审核。 |
| 17 | Back to the Purchase Orders grid, slow zoom out. | That is it. Confirm your orders, keep dates and shipments up to date, download your labels from the portal, and add tracking to every sample. If you have any questions, message us from the portal. Thank you. | 以上就是全部内容。请确认订单，保持日期和发运信息最新，从门户下载标签，并为每个样品填写追踪号。如有任何问题，请通过门户联系我们。谢谢！ |

## Notes for Ben
- Scene 15: the portal does not have a "ships on Dock & Bay's account" option and tracking is not required by the system; the narration asks for it. Say if you want the portal to require it.
- Scene 5 only appears on POs where Dock & Bay has turned on "require confirmation"; the demo PO will have it on.
- Ship date and status save straight away (no approval); completion date goes to Dock & Bay for approval.
