## ⚠️ Buy-plan / forecast behaviour changes — please spot-check buy quantities

This batch reworked how SKU forecasts are derived when there's no saved override, and how urgent/first buys size. Net effect is mostly on the **forward tail and edge cases**, but it touches buy numbers — worth a sanity pass after merge.

- **Forecast basis (v26.452 → v26.467):** no-override SKUs now fall back to a **chained last-year rule** — last-year *actuals* where the period is complete, last-year *forecast* where it isn't (v26.461); the flat-LY basis is the **trailing 12 actual months**, not the 2025 calendar year (v26.454). New/continuing SKUs with `products.replacement_sku` set now **inherit the predecessor's sales history in-app** and are treated as continuing (v26.467) — the old Apps-Script pre-stamp is gone and n8n does not replicate it, hence the in-app inference.
- **Complex Rules (v26.418 → v26.421):** new cover-target override engine replaces **First Buy**, which has been removed (Phase 3). Reads `buy_complex_rules` (mig 170). Changes buy qty for matched SKUs during their windows.
- **Urgent buy (v26.423):** fires below **2 weeks** cover and sizes to demand with no buffer; never rush-restocks a SKU's discontinue month (v26.417).
- **Auto Forecast (v26.438 → v26.441):** rolling monthly buy plan (net stock + open POs), window extended 12 → up to 18 months, cost = forecast-weighted current product cost.

> Transfers (Rebalance/Urgent) are a **display/report overlay only** — they do **not** feed buy quantities.
