## PROD DIVERGENCE — one edit inside your restructured `PO_ROWS_SQL` (v27.903)

Ben's `PO_ROWS_SQL` reads from `planner.v_po_finance`; the view is **untouched** (no migration). The ERP delivery-date drift flag is now sourced from the Fulfil mirror first. Your prod query needs the same three pieces wherever the equivalent lives:

1. Join the mirror to the PO row: `LEFT JOIN planner.fulfil_purchase_orders fm ON fm.po = v.po`
2. Expose `coalesce(fm.requested_delivery_date, fm.delivery_date, v.erp_final_delivery_date) AS erp_final_delivery_eff` and `(fm.po IS NOT NULL) AS erp_is_fulfil`
3. Use `erp_final_delivery_eff` in place of `erp_final_delivery_date` for the `erp_final_delivery` output column **and** inside the `erp_date_pending` CASE. Add `erp_is_fulfil` to the PO-row field whitelist.

Ben's reference lines (server.mjs): 17495, 17500, 17621, 17625; whitelist ~17702. Why: the view joins the Cin7-era `erp_purchase_orders` row, frozen since June. PO-57EUXR1 showed "ERP final delivery 12-Oct" while Fulfil held 26-Oct. The Payments-tab banner now names its source ("Fulfil PO delivery date" vs "ERP final delivery (Cin7 mirror)").

Nothing else in this batch touches your marked divergences (ORDER_PLAN_SELECT, root handler, hzUpload). The v27.881 child-PO cache shape you kept is unaffected.
