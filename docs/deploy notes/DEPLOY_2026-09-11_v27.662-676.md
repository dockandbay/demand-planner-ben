## ⚠️ Two things to verify on deploy
- **v27.666 — access-gate change (AUTH).** The legacy `PLANNER_KEY` gate now lets a request through if it carries a **forwarded auth email** (SSO/proxy-authenticated), even without the shared `pk` cookie — the per-user edit-guard (`permsFor`) still enforces SUPPLY/DEMAND/admin rights. This fixes permissioned users (e.g. zera@, admin) getting a blanket **"unauthorised"** on writes when their shared-key cookie was missing/expired. **Assumes the prod auth proxy sets/strips the forwarded-email header** (the whole permission model already relies on this) — please confirm.

Fix baked the base tokens into `portal.html` as a zero-specificity `:where(:root)` fallback (portal now renders correctly regardless). **Please still confirm the live portal head carries + loads `<link rel="stylesheet" href="/hz-theme.css?v=…">`** — otherwise the portal also misses hz-theme's shell rules, not just tokens.
