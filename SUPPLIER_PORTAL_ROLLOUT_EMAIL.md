**Subject: Introducing the Dock & Bay Supplier Portal — one shared place for all our orders**

Hi [Supplier name],

**👉 Visit and log in at: suppliers.dockandbay.com/portal**

We're moving all of our order management into a single online **Supplier Portal**. The goal is simple: **shared visibility of every order we process with you, and one collaborative place to manage the information** — so we can move away from scattered spreadsheets and WeChat messages, and everyone works from the same live, accurate data.

**Why we're doing this**
- You'll see **all your orders in one place**, always up to date — no more chasing versions of spreadsheets.
- We both work from the **same information**, so fewer errors and misunderstandings.
- All updates, documents and messages live **against the specific order** — nothing gets lost in chat.

**How to get started**
- Go to **suppliers.dockandbay.com/portal**.
- Enter **the email address we have on file for you** and request a login link.
- Click the **magic link** we email you to log in — no password to remember.
- You'll only ever see **your own orders and data**.

**What you need to do**

Please keep the following data up to date against each order — this is the information we rely on:

- **Open actions** — the portal flags what needs your attention on each order; work through these first and keep them cleared.
- **Production completion date** — confirm the expected completion date of each PO, and mark an order/batch **complete** when goods are ready.
- **Shipment tracking + freight cost** — enter the tracking code and freight charge against each order. **Samples and air shipments go from your own DHL/FedEx account** — log the freight cost and tracking code per order so the charge comes straight to us, not buried in a spreadsheet.
- **Invoice** — upload and submit your invoice against the relevant PO.
- **Cost prices** — enter your actual line costs and any additional charges (we'll review anything that differs from our plan).
- **Timeline — communicate with us here** — use the order's timeline to message us and attach files, so all discussion stays against the right order. **This replaces WeChat for anything order-related.**

**Where to find things**
- **Purchase Orders** — your live orders, grouped by production batch.
- **Shipment Plan** — shipments by destination, including FOB. Here you can **download shipment labels** (and **crossdock labels once your shipment is allocated**), and see how shipments are **consolidated** — i.e. which supplier will consolidate your shipment, or which other suppliers' POs are being consolidated onto your shipment.
- **Payments** — amounts, payment types and references.
- **Productions** — full order plan per batch, downloadable. You can also **self-serve download product and carton barcodes** for each batch.
- **Samples** — request and track sample orders, and log the **DHL/FedEx freight cost and tracking code** for samples you ship from your own account.

**Can't log in?** If your email isn't recognised or the link doesn't arrive, please contact **Zera** or **[Ben]** and we'll get you set up.
